Tokeni Bar for Windows includes the SQLite command-line shell.

SQLite version: 3.53.4
Official archive: https://www.sqlite.org/2026/sqlite-tools-win-x64-3530400.zip
SQLite copyright and public-domain notice: https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html

SQLite is in the public domain. The official SQLite copyright page states that
the deliverable code may be copied, modified, published, used, compiled, sold,
or distributed for commercial or non-commercial purposes.

Only sqlite3.exe from the official Windows x64 tools archive is redistributed.
The archive version and cryptographic digests are pinned in sqlite-tools.json.
